The files included here were used for part of the anonymisation pipeline
- IMAGE_CROPPER --> Used to remove parts of screenshots from participants' screen that showed anything personal (e.g., browser tab names)
- VOICE_ANONYMISATION --> Used to perform voice conversion on audio files

Other anonymisation steps were carried out manually:
- Bleeping names on audio files done using Audacity
- Silencing names on video files done with DaVinci Resolve
- Blurring video frames and covering any personal details on video files done with DaVinci Resolve
- Deleting screenshots where most of the information was personal (i.e., cropping is not enough) done manually (e.g., screenshots of Desktop)
- Deleting names in survey responses done in Excel
- Video voice filtering (pitch, timbre, noise and distortion effects) done with DaVinci Resolve
